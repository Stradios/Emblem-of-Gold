# Emblem of Gold

**Emblem of Gold** is a simple World of Warcraft 3.3.5a (WOTLK) addon that shows your emblem counts — Heroism, Valor, Conquest, Triumph and Frost — inside the vendor window, right next to your gold.

### 📸 Screenshot

<img src="https://user.uploads.dev/file/092ab4cf80d6040a241a757c001160bd.png" alt="Emblem of Gold - emblem counts displayed next to the gold in the vendor window" width="350">

### 📦 Features

- Emblem counts shown **inside the vendor window**, on the same row as your money
- Sized to match the gold display — the number font and coin icon size are read from the money frame at runtime
- Compact icons with counts
- Hover the badges for a tooltip with the full emblem names
- Updates when you open the vendor and when you buy something
- No separate box under the vendor, and no duplicate gold display


### 🧩 Installation

1. Download or clone this repo
2. Place the folder `EmblemOfGold` into your WoW `Interface/AddOns/` directory
3. Restart WoW or `/reload`
